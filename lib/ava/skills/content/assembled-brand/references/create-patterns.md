# Create patterns

Text patterns for AVA. The app or a designer places them on the template. Write the words. Do not write Python, file paths or image URLs. Photos and logos come from get_brand_assets.

## Case study

Title states the result, sentence case, full stop, one serif italic phrase.

Then two short columns:
- Insight tag, then what was true before the work.
- Action tag, then what was done.

Close with an Outcome card: one hero number that is already in the source, the unit, and the source line under it. If the client logo is needed, call get_brand_assets. If it is not in the catalogue, leave a labelled slot. Never invent the number.

## Big numbers

One row of two to four stats. Each stat is:
- the figure, already in the source
- a short label
- the source in muted type

On a black ground the figures are lime. On white they are forest. One Outcome tag for the row. No second headline.

## Data slide

Title is the finding, as a sentence with a full stop.

Then a table or labelled bars: exact figures from the source, forest for the series that is the point, sky when the series is an insight, grey for context. No gridlines. Values sit on the bars.

Under the table, a sand insight card: Insight tag, then one sentence that says so what. The sentence may not introduce a new figure.

## Insight card

A filled sand card. Insight tag in sky. One sentence. No chart inside the card. If the page also has an action, that is a separate forest tag, not a second sentence crammed into the card.
