### Campaign MBA layout

`CampaignPageAssembly` (`app/dashboard/[slug]/[mba_number]/components/CampaignPageAssembly.tsx`) is the compositor. Sole production mount: `app/dashboard/[slug]/[mba_number]/page.tsx`. Section order:

2. **Campaign read** (`CampaignReadSection`) — clients see a published read only (six beats + “Read as at”); admins also get draft/publish/regenerate/history. Empty: admin “No read yet” + Regenerate; clients see nothing. Hero “Get AVA's read” triggers this section's Regenerate. Generate is `POST /api/campaign-reads/generate` → 202 + `generating`; `after()` continues the job on the same instance. The section polls GET every 3s for up to 3 minutes (`credentials: "include"`, `cache: "no-store"`) and shows “Writing the read…”, then “Still writing, refresh to check”. Generate and GET fail `generating` rows older than 10 minutes (`Timed out`). A failed row shows `error_message` + Regenerate only when it is the newest row for that mba and version; an older failure stays in history and is not shown. A draft older than seven days shows “Read as at {date} · Out of date.” on the header, keeps Regenerate and the other controls, hides the beats until Edit, and sits below Where we are and the I/A/O row. A published read stays above Planned audience. It is not regenerated on its own. Generation tools are campaign-scoped (no `get_pacing_snapshot`). Delivery lines carry `delivery_state` (including `spend_only`); What was planned uses the live-line budget sum; Against the plan copies the strip expected resolver; KPI beats follow the review card resolver.
3. Planned audience
4. **Where we are** (`CampaignStatusStrip`; delivered bar follows the pacing tone, expected and time elapsed are forest, impressions are sky). `CampaignInsightCards` sits under the strip with the page section gap (`space-y-6`). It is the latest `GET /api/insights` row for the MBA with action or outcome set (`IAOCards`); the action owner is its own “Owner:” line. Hidden when there is none or the caller is not admin. After that block, when `FEATURE_CREATIVE_PERFORMANCE` is on (every client), `TopCreativeStrip` sits before Channels at a glance. It is not a `SectionBoundary` and is not in the section-order list. One fetch feeds the strip and every delivery line. `LineItemBlock` shows that line's ads beside the daily chart, stacked under it below 900px. A line with no creative rows adds nothing. Loading, 503 and error stay on the strip. `preview.status` `none` is the format frame.
5. **Channels at a glance** (`ChannelsAtAGlance`) — omitted when `visibleCoverageCards` is empty. A card's main line is delivered of schedule expected to date, then planned in total. The bar is delivered ÷ planned, with a marker at expected ÷ planned. Status is `deliveryStatusFromPct` of delivered ÷ expected. Every schedule channel with expected above 0 has a card, including offline channels. No source and no assumed spend shows "No source" and no pace pill. Expected 0 with a future start shows "Not started". Cards are ordered by expected, largest first.
6. Admin-only: Team hours (`CampaignHoursWidget`, `GET /api/codex/time/summary`) → **KPI review** (`KpiReview`, `lib/kpi/kpiReview.ts`; admins only, when `shouldShowKpiReview` sees at least one card) → Recent insights
7. Delivery (`CampaignDeliverySection` accordion)
8. Media plan (`MediaPlanVizSection`)
9. **The plan** (`SpendChartsRow`)

**CampaignStatusStrip** replaces the old Progress summary / `CampaignSummaryRow` verdict line (`CampaignSummaryRow` stays in the tree for helper tests; not mounted). Left: status pill + `statusSentence`. Right: Delivered to date / Expected by now / Impressions / Time elapsed. Pill is `deliveryStatusFromPct` of delivered ÷ the schedule expected the card prints (behind below 90, on track 90–110, ahead above 110, and 115 and above still Ahead). Portfolio over-pacing is the same percent at 115 via `isOverPacing`. `campaignPacingVerdict` stays for `CampaignSummaryRow` and is not the pill. Delivered to date is "—" when `actualSpend` is unset (impressions-only, same gate as `page.tsx` `hasReportedDeliveredSpend`). **Impressions hide when `hasDelivery` is false.** Coverage (`lib/delivery/channelCoverage.ts`) supplies `channelsReporting` / `channelsTotal` / ahead-channel into the strip; planned deliverables are summed from each group's section `progressCards` "Delivered X · Planned Y" (not the raw line `impressions` field — CPV planned views live there). Direct-digital groups (`digital-display` / `digital-video` / `digital-audio` / `bvod`) read the impressions card at `progressCards[0]` (CM360 clicks sit at `[1]` — that 100 is not delivered impressions). Other families still read the deliverable card at `[1]`. When matching line ids exist, delivered and planned impressions sum those line captions. The strip caption is delivered / that sum. Glance noun stays Impressions unless every card in the group is CPV, then Views.

**`statusSentence`** (`lib/dashboard/statusSentence.ts`) — first match of a–d, with an unknown-spend gate after **a**:

- **a.** `channelsTotal` known, `channelsReporting < channelsTotal`, and `spendPct < 0.5` → "{n} of {total} channels are reporting so far, which is why delivered spend looks low." If `aheadChannelName` is set (first reporting channel whose glance card status is ahead — spend, or deliverable when spend is hidden), append " {Name} is ahead." Omit the clause when none are ahead on that measure.
- **unknown spend.** When `actualSpend` is unset, skip b–d and return "No delivery reported yet." Never treat missing spend as $0 in the behind-by-$X gap.
- **b.** `impressionsPct > spendPct + 0.15` and actual CPM below planned → cheaper-than-planned sentence.
- **c.** behind / over-pacing / critical → "Spend is behind the plan by {gap}. See the channel cards below for where."
- **d.** "Delivery is on track against the plan."

Strip "Day N of M" uses Melbourne civil-calendar inclusive counts (`inclusiveCampaignDayMetrics` / `getMelbourneTodayISO`) — not the host timezone wall clock. `getDeliveredTotalsForCampaign` passes a non-empty `lineItemsMap` into `loadDeliverySnapshot` as `lineItemsByChannel` so the strip uses the same Postgres plan lines as the channel cards; otherwise the snapshot loads via `fetchAllPlanLineItemsForDelivery` (plans backend). **Coverage statuses** (`ChannelCoverageStatus`): `reporting` (≥1 PACING_FACT / chart daily row) · `connecting` (a delivery source exists — `delivery_source_map` row or social classifier — but no fact rows yet, and the earliest burst is not after Melbourne today) · `not_started` (source exists, earliest burst after today) · `no_source` (no map row / classifier).
